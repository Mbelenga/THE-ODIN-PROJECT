Just A Reminder

The sun dipped below the horizon, casting a warm golden hue across the quiet village. Birds chirped softly as they settled into their nests, while a gentle breeze rustled through the tall grass. Children’s laughter echoed faintly in the distance, their playful games winding down as night began to fall. The aroma of freshly baked bread wafted through the air, mingling with the faint scent of blooming jasmine.

In a small house by the edge of the woods, an elderly man sat by a crackling fireplace, a book open on his lap. The room was cozy, filled with the memories of a life well-lived—pictures on the mantle, a worn armchair by the window, and a clock ticking softly on the wall. He sipped his tea slowly, savoring the quiet moments of solitude, while the world outside fell into a peaceful slumber.
